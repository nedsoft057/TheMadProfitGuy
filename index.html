How I think

Three things decide it.

If you want to be successful in business, these are the three essentials you must attend to.

01

Your finances

You will go out of business if you don't know how to manage your money. Your business depends on how you handle your cash.

02

Your people

Empower others to work for you. Hire into documented systems, set deal-breakers early, and lead with empathy.

03

Your customers

Make the value visible. Points on the receipt, alerts in real time, and dignity in every interaction.
